# Video Script · B-001 C&I BESS Certifications — Make the Model Number Match

| 时间 | 口播(EN) | 画面建议 | 字幕要点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5s(HOOK) | You have the power rating and the delivery date. Then the grid operator asks one question: *the certificate for this exact unit?* | 特写:采购手里一叠文件,停在一张证书上;镜头推近证书上的"型号/Model"栏 | "Certificate for THIS exact unit?" |
| 5–15s(问题) | Too many C&I projects stall here. Not on the hardware — on the paper. A certificate is not a marketing badge; it is an evidence chain. | 动画:一摞证书 + 打叉的公司级声明;切"项目停摆"的进度条画面 | "Stalled by the paper, not the product" |
| 15–30s(方案方向) | Your reviewer needs three links: the standard, the certificate, and the model on the nameplate. All three must point at the unit you are actually buying. | 三步链条动画:标准 → 证书 → 铭牌型号;三者对齐亮起 | "Standard → Certificate → Nameplate model" |
| 30–50s(证明/落地 1) | Start with safety: cells and battery systems to IEC 62619:2022 [F-CERTIFICATION-001], plus the ESS supplement IEC 63056:2020 [F-CERTIFICATION-002]. And because standards get revised, the edition must be stated [F-CERTIFICATION-001]. | 证书翻页特写,高亮"IEC 62619:2022"与"版本/edition";旁注"看标准 + 看版本" | "IEC 62619:2022 · IEC 63056:2020" |
| 50–70s(证明/落地 2) | Then logistics and market access: UN 38.3 for lithium-battery transport [F-CERTIFICATION-004], CE self-declared to EN IEC 62619/63056 [F-CERTIFICATION-003]. Grid connection is a separate, local step — e.g. VDE-AR-N 4105 on the integrator side [F-CERTIFICATION-005]. | 分屏:左侧"运输+CE"图标(UN38.3/CE);右侧电网图标标"项目侧/集成商";最后叠 MiraSol DB-100 柜体画面 | "UN 38.3 · CE → market access; Grid code = local step" |
| 70–85s(落地核对) | The decisive check: does the model on the certificate match the nameplate of the unit on your order? For DB-100 [F-PRODUCT-001], DB-200 [F-PRODUCT-005] or DB-1000 [F-PRODUCT-009] — all liquid-cooled [F-PRODUCT-002][F-PRODUCT-006][F-PRODUCT-010] — hold the document trail next to the order. | 实物比对:证书与柜体铭牌并排,放大镜对型号;三款产品(DB-100/200/1000)快速过场 | "Match the model on the certificate ↔ nameplate" |
| 85–90s(CTA) | Ask us to walk you through the package, model by model. Comment "cert-pack" for the checklist. No quote or commitment here — figures confirmed by a person in a follow-up [F-COMPANY-004]. | 结尾卡:MiraSol 示例厂牌 + "评论区:cert-pack";放一张"五步核对清单"封面 | "Comment 'cert-pack' · Reviewed by a person, no auto-quote" |
