Your specs are approved — until the reviewer asks for the certificate of *that exact unit*.

For a behind-the-meter C&I battery, "certified" is not a marketing badge. It is an evidence chain: the standard, the certificate, the model on the nameplate. If the last link does not name the unit you are ordering, the folder does not close the case.

What a credible certificate pack includes:

- **Cell and battery-system safety:** IEC 62619:2022 [F-CERTIFICATION-001], plus the storage-system supplement IEC 63056:2020 [F-CERTIFICATION-002].
- **European market access:** CE mark, self-declared to EN IEC 62619 / EN IEC 63056 [F-CERTIFICATION-003], and lithium-battery transport to UN 38.3 [F-CERTIFICATION-004]. Together they cover the common EN/DE market-access requirements [F-CERTIFICATION-006].
- **Grid connection stays out of the factory folder:** codes such as VDE-AR-N 4105 are integrator-side, project-level compliance [F-CERTIFICATION-005].

We hold each model's document trail next to the order — DB-100 [F-PRODUCT-001], DB-200 [F-PRODUCT-005] and DB-1000 [F-PRODUCT-009], all on one liquid-cooled platform [F-PRODUCT-002][F-PRODUCT-006][F-PRODUCT-010].

Ask for the document that names your model before you file the pack. Comment "cert-pack" and we will walk you through the checklist, model by model.
